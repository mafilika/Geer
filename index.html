About Solid Rock Construction

Craftsmanship that gives your project a stronger foundation.

Solid Rock Construction provides dependable construction services for homeowners, property owners and businesses. Whether you’re building something new, renovating an existing space, or repairing and upgrading a property, we work with you from the first conversation through to the finished result.

Our team handles building and construction, roofing, plumbing, tiling, ceilings and aluminium windows and doors — so you can deal with one contractor who understands how these trades work together, rather than coordinating several on your own.
